How the radar behaves on the real board: after switching to Radar it calibrates for 5 seconds (keep the movement slider at 0 during that time). Then any movement between the router and the board turns the ring red and sends ripples out from a glowing core. Faster ripples and a bigger core mean stronger motion. With one board and one router, Wi-Fi CSI can tell that something moved and how strongly, but not where, so nothing on screen marks a position.

Text is rendered from a system font, so letters look slightly different from the real display's built-in font.
